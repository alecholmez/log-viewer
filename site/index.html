Know what to change in the tune.

A virtual dyno for ECU logs. It estimates wheel power from each pull and lists what to change, with numbers.

Download View on GitHub

Each finding says what to change

Evidence from your log, why it matters, and numbered steps with values worked out from your data. Risks are listed first.

See the data behind each finding

A finding opens the replay or the table it was read from, at the samples it describes.

Getting logs in

Logs are copied into the app’s own library, so the original can be moved or deleted afterwards.

Add logs
Pick one file or many. A log that is already in the library is not added twice.
Drag and drop
Drop files on the window, or open a CSV with Log Viewer.
Watch folder
Point the app at a synced folder. New logs from the tuning laptop are added when the app opens.
On your device
Everything is read on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
